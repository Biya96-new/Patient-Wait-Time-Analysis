# 🏥 Patient Wait Time Analysis – Hospital Operations Dashboard

## 🏢 Business Context

Patient wait time is one of the most critical indicators of healthcare efficiency and service quality. Long waiting times not only impact patient satisfaction but can also affect treatment outcomes and hospital operational capacity.

This project analyzes **July 2026 hospital visit data (800 patient records)** to identify bottlenecks in patient flow, department efficiency, triage effectiveness, and hospital-level performance.

The goal is to uncover operational inefficiencies and provide data-driven recommendations to improve patient experience and hospital efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Evaluate overall patient wait time across hospitals
- Compare wait times across hospitals, departments, and triage levels
- Identify bottlenecks in emergency care and critical departments
- Analyze weekly performance trends across hospitals
- Detect high-risk operational segments
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve efficiency

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Excel

---

## 📈 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of patient flow efficiency through:

- **Overview** → Total patients, average wait time
- **Hospital Analysis** → Wait time comparison across hospitals
- **Department Analysis** → Emergency vs OPD vs other departments
- **Triage Analysis** → High, Medium, Low priority patient flow
- **Weekly Trend Analysis** → Operational performance over time
- **Risk Segmentation** → High bottleneck combinations (Hospital + Department)

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

- Total Patients
- Average Wait Time
- Wait Time by Hospital
- Wait Time by Department
- Wait Time by Triage Level
- Weekly Trend in Wait Time

- **800 patients** were analyzed with an overall **average wait time of 30 minutes**

- In critical cases, Emergency in :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} reached nearly **60 minutes**

---

## 🚨 Critical Bottleneck

**Hospital C + Emergency Department → ~60 minutes average wait time**

This represents the most critical operational issue in the dataset.

Emergency care delays can significantly impact patient outcomes, making this a high-priority area for intervention.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Strengthen staffing and response time in Emergency departments
- Improve triage-based patient routing to reduce overlapping queues
- Standardize triage prioritization to improve segregation of patient queues

---

## 📍 Business Value

- Identified key operational bottlenecks in patient flow
- Highlighted high-risk hospital-department combinations
- Provided visibility into weekly efficiency trends
- Enabled data-driven decisions for hospital resource planning
- Improved understanding of emergency department workload challenges

---

## 🚀 Key Takeaway

Hospital performance is not defined only by average wait times — it also reflects how consistently operations improve and sustain efficiency over time.
